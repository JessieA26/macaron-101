# 03. Ingredient Prep, Resting & Baking Thermodynamics

Even perfect meringue ([01. Meringue Methods](01-meringue-methods.md)) and folding ([02. Macaronage Folding](02-macaronage-folding.md)) will fail without proper drying and oven heat control `[Source 5: Sally's Baking Addiction]`.

## 1. Critical Ingredient Preparation
- **Weigh Ingredients in Grams:** Volume measurements (cups) cause inconsistent ratios; always use a digital scale `[Source 3: King Arthur Baking]`, `[Source 5: Sally's Baking Addiction]`.
- **Sift Dry Ingredients:** Sifting almond flour and powdered sugar removes coarse almond bits that cause lumpy shells `[Source 4: Pastreez]`, `[Source 5: Sally's Baking Addiction]`.
- **Room-Temperature / Aged Egg Whites:** Room-temperature whites whip to greater volume than cold whites `[Source 5: Sally's Baking Addiction]`.

## 2. Piping & Resting ("Skin" Formation)
- **Pipe Vertically & Tap Trays:** Hold piping bag perpendicular (90°) to the baking sheet, then rap the tray firmly against the counter 2–3 times to pop trapped air bubbles `[Source 5: Sally's Baking Addiction]`.
- **Why Resting Matters:** Leaving piped shells at room temperature for **30–60 minutes** dries the outer surface into a dull, non-sticky skin `[Source 4: Pastreez]`, `[Source 5: Sally's Baking Addiction]`.
- **The Physics of "Feet":** When baked, the dry top skin blocks steam from escaping upward, forcing steam out the bottom edge—lifting the shell straight up to form ruffled "feet" `[Source 5: Sally's Baking Addiction]`.

## 3. Baking Temperature & Oven Control
- **Low & Steady Heat:** Macarons bake at relatively low temperatures (**~140°C–160°C / 285°F–325°F**) for **12–16 minutes** so the interior cooks without browning the delicate tops `[Source 5: Sally's Baking Addiction]`.
- **Doneness Test:** Gently nudge the top of a shell—if it wobbles on its feet, bake 1–2 minutes longer; if it feels firmly attached, it is done `[Source 5: Sally's Baking Addiction]`.

## Conflicts & Verification Notes
- **Resting Time & Oven Temp Sensitivity:** `[Source 4: Pastreez]` highlights that humid environments extend resting time significantly, while `[Source 5: Sally's Baking Addiction]` notes every oven runs differently (recommending an oven thermometer around 300°F–325°F / 150°C–160°C, whereas Italian meringue recipes often bake slightly lower around 140°C–150°C).
